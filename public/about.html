Fewer things. Chosen properly.

PetPaw Haven started with a simple idea: pet products should be useful, reliable and worth having around. We look for products that solve everyday problems for pet owners rather than adding another gimmick to the house.

Our range is kept small on purpose. Every product has a reason to be here, whether it makes feeding easier, keeps your pet hydrated or simply makes everyday pet care a little more convenient.

We're based in South Africa and aim to make ordering simple from start to finish. Orders are placed securely through PayFast and are delivered to addresses within South Africa.

Shipping

Free shipping on all orders in South Africa.

Once your order has been confirmed and payment has been received, it will be prepared for delivery. Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days, depending on your location and the courier, but we'll always use the delivery details you provide at checkout.

You'll receive your order at the address supplied during checkout, so please make sure your delivery details and contact information are correct before placing your order.

Need help?

If you have a question about an order, delivery, product or anything else, just get in touch with us through our Contact page. We'd rather help sort out a problem than leave you wondering what's happening with your order.

Returns & Issues

If there's a problem with your order or the product you received, please contact us as soon as possible with your order details and a description of the issue. We'll review it and get back to you with the next steps.
